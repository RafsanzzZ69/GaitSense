## Information for the participant

We are studying whether a phone camera can measure patterns in walking. This is
a student research study, not a medical examination. It does not diagnose
conditions, predict falls or recommend treatment. There may be no direct benefit.

If you agree, we will record short videos of you walking comfortably along a
clear marked path, usually three trials with rest as needed. You may refuse any
trial, stop immediately or withdraw without penalty or effect on grades/services.
Do not continue if uncomfortable. No deliberately altered or weighted walking is
requested. Risks include ordinary walking discomfort and loss of video privacy.

Video may show your face/body and may identify you. Numerical movement features
can also be sensitive; participant codes reduce identification but do not make
data guaranteed anonymous. Authorized study staff will use private storage, not
public GitHub or the public GaitSense website. No automatic cloud upload is part
of this collection protocol.

By default, video is deleted after extraction and reference annotation/QC under
the approved schedule; it is not kept indefinitely. For app use, the intended
default is deletion after successful extraction. These are different workflows.
The final approved form must state exact retention deadlines for temporary video,
features, consent records and backups, and what happens if extraction fails.

You can request withdrawal using your participant code and the researcher contact.
Identifiable/pseudonymous records will be removed according to the approved
policy; already published aggregate results may not be retractable. Explain the
actual withdrawal deadline before signing. Public release requires specific
separate permission; participation alone never authorizes it.
